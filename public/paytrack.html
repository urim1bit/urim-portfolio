QUESTIONS & CLARITY

Frequently asked questions.

schedule How does overtime calculation work?

You can set custom overtime multipliers (such as 1.5x or 2.0x) per shift or per work profile. PayTrack automatically multiplies your base hourly rate by your overtime hours and adds any shift allowances.

work Can I track multiple jobs at once?

Yes. Create separate Work Profiles for each employer or contract, each with its own hourly rate, currency symbol, break rules, and pay period schedule.

wifi_off Does PayTrack require an internet connection?

Never. PayTrack is 100% offline-first. All logged shifts, work profiles, and earnings analytics are stored locally on your device.

description Can I export my logged hours for my manager?

Absolutely. Generate clean PDF shift summaries or Excel spreadsheets for any date range and share them in one tap via WhatsApp, Gmail, or Drive.
